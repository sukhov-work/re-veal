# Camera-move sheet — 2026-09-23 (TR10 built as `transitions.py pair --camera flat|ramp|model --zoom`)

Every clip: preset `morph`, 2 s (60 frames), canvas capped at 1920 px, zoom 0.25 where a camera is named. Run dir `benchmarks/runs/2026-09-23/` (gitignored): `index.html` is the review page with a pick and a note per pair; `matched/index.html` is the matched-pair comparison page. Numbers from `report.json` (the quality basket on a 480-px proxy). `disp` = median displacement of the A→B field in canvas px; `cert` = `mean_certainty` (under a camera move: the mean depth importance); `step` = mean / max adjacent-frame difference in levels; `we` = warping error; `er` = `edge_ratio` (hidden-cut detector); `corr s` / `render s` = correspondence (incl. the depth model under `model`) and frame rendering wall time.

## Reading (the agent's, 2026-09-23; the owner's eye decides)
- On the six mismatched pairs at zoom 0.25 the model's median displacement is 26–46 % below flat (the disparity means are 0.12–0.43, so the mean factor `0.5 + d` is below 1), the mean step is 4–14 % lower, the warping error is equal or lower on five of six, and `edge_ratio` moves within ±0.06 except mismatch_2 (0.19 → 0.24) and mismatch_6 (0.53 → 0.59; the outlier under `flat` too). `ramp` sits between the two. The model costs 2.4–2.9 s of correspondence per pair (a cold process: about 1.2 s of model load plus two images) against 0.08–0.45 s for `flat`; rendering is unchanged.
- Run 2 reproduces run 1 byte for byte (mp4 md5) on all six mismatches under `model`.
- Forced class B on the six class A pairs replaces the correspondence with the camera move and a crossfade: the mean step rises to 2.1–10.6 levels against the class A morph's 0.5–5.3 (match_4: 8.8 against 1.5). Whether the move adds to a matched pair or fights the morph is the owner's call on `matched/index.html`.
- Every push-in clip scores `edge_ratio` 1.1–1.8, above the hidden-cut line of 1: the sin(πu) amplitude has its steepest slope at the endpoints, so the first and last frame steps are the largest of the clip. If the owner wants the push-in, the onset needs a flatter curve (sin² or an eased ramp) before it can be an option. Depth against flat push-in differs by at most 1.9 levels of mean step (match_4 10.4 against 12.3).
