## 0. The decision in one paragraph

Build `scripts/research/studio.py`: one research script, a stdlib server on 127.0.0.1:8380 (8379 is the workbench's, which stays live through phase B), one embedded vanilla-JavaScript page, no build step, no framework; the UI process opens no connection of its own, and a subprocess it starts opens one only on an explicit warmup click or for the box's `ssh` (§5); the job folder on disk is the only state; every heavy call is a subprocess of the existing scripts (`reveal.py align`, `transitions.py pair`, `scripts/research/director_render.py`, `scripts/research/loop_seq.py`, the model helpers). The page shows every job as a **strip of stages** (one tile per process the operator used to run by hand or by script: import, readers, registration, probe, plan, render, measures, skeptic, page, grades), each tile opening into the stage's panel with its intermediates, knobs and "rerun from here". Four flows share that shell: align (Reveal's own features), a pair transition, a director's plan, a loop from a sequence. The agent session stays in the loop through request files (the planner, the skeptic, the readings, a keyframe on the Strix Halo box). The visual world is `DESIGN.md` ("the light table"). The studio is a full rewrite (his answer of 2026-11-04 evening: "Retire existing one, do a full rewrite"): the workbench's lessons (the job folder, the subprocess runner, the progress channel, cancel, the Fable review's restart defect T60) carry over as rules, not as code; `director_ui.py` is retired when the studio covers the director flow (phase B). Reversible: a new file and a new port; neither tool changes. Amended after two skeptic lenses the same day: §12.

## 1. The brief (impeccable `shape`, confirmed by the owner's words; the open decisions are in §10)

**Job and audience.** One user, the owner, at a 27-inch display, in the Operate mode: he comes to make a clip from his own photographs and to judge it. He wants "full control over everything from UI", every process "under the hood" shown "visually", tunable, "regardless on type of match / mismatch", "with external and internal models, optional halo box support", and "original reveal features" (his order, 2026-11-04). Earlier words that bind: "UI with all tweaks and all the tools for director, loading progress, maybe ability to annotate part of pics, play with layering" (2026-10-20); "a tunable part of our main app and UI (separate additional flow)" for the loops (2026-10-30); "never overfit on specific pictures or flows" (2026-10-20).

**Outcome and proof.** He runs a sequence or a pair from the folder to a graded page without the session, except where the session is the model (the planner, the skeptic). Proof: the acceptance checks of §9, each a scripted drive of the real surface with the angel sequence and the mismatch_3 pair, plus his eye on the first screens.

**Selected direction.** The light table (`DESIGN.md`): dark neutral surfaces, one amber accent, states as chips with words. The structural thesis: **every process is a strip of frames**; the signature move: the strip across the top of every job, and under every render **a shared frame axis** that ties the scrubber, the step series, the object-stability plot and the skeptic's markers to the same x (Track C §9). Standard web controls only.

**Scope and boundaries.** Fidelity: a production screen for one user, not a demo. Breadth: the four flows and the shared stages (§2). Interactivity: everything the operator turned by hand in the sessions since 2026-10-21 (Track B §B, §E). Untouched: `reveal.py` and `transitions.py` (invariant 7, T68: nothing enters either tool without his word); the research scripts' CLIs stay the backends and gain only default-off flags (§7); the frozen plans under `scripts/research/plans/` are never written. Anti-goals: a JavaScript framework or build step; a UI that opens a network connection; a modal where an inline panel serves; a second visual world per flow. A free-form node canvas is not an anti-goal any more: his answer 4 asks for "visualization of the flow, maybe some graph, also think of node/prop based workflow editing like we have in blender or nuke"; §3 adds the graph view beside the strip.

**States and ranges.** Jobs per day 1–20; stills per sequence 2–10 (the boxes repaint had 7); a round 1–10 jobs; a render 25–300 frames at 25 fps; candidates per round 1–24 (Halloween had 24); a draft 5–60 s, a full render 30 s–10 min (the seasons' s7 at 3522 px took 3 min), a box keyframe 40 s–30 min; a plan 1–40 keys; a job folder 50 MB–2 GB. Material states: a job with nothing run (the strip is grey tiles with names); a stage running (progress from the log); a stage failed or refused (Reveal refuses an unrelated pair with exit 2: the tile shows "refused" and the reason); a stage lost after a restart (reconciled from the files); a render unjudged (no skeptic answer yet); a candidate graded; the box held (`~/halo-hold` present) or unreachable.

**Interaction and layout.** The shell of `DESIGN.md §Layout`: a left rail (jobs, running tasks, models), the stage strip across the top of the work area, the selected stage's panel beneath, a right inspector (the selected thing's knobs, measures, provenance). Keyboard: the strip and the scrubber are arrow-steppable; every control has a visible focus. Feedback: a running stage shows its progress number and the log's last lines, never a spinner; a finished stage's thumbnail appears in its tile.

**Constraints.** 127.0.0.1 only; the job folder as state; subprocesses only; the originals never copied or modified (symlinks and sha256s); every render records the plan's sha256, the script's md5 and the outputs' md5s; reproducibility is a watch, not a hope.

The registry is a Python table in `studio.py`: stage name → the argv template of the script that runs it → the files it writes → the knobs it takes (name, type, range, default, the sentence that explains it) → the visual the panel draws. The build copies the knob sentences from the scripts' `--help` and `SCHEMA.md`, never invents a range (`/reveal` skill, Phase 2 rule 1).
```
benchmarks/runs/<date>/studio/<job_id>/        (gitignored under /benchmarks/runs/)
  job.json             {id, flow: align|pair|director|loop, created, ui_version, inputs: [{path (the symlink target), sha256, exif}], order}
  in/                  symlinks to the originals (never copies); the registered stills, prep.json, depth_k*.npy (a loop)
  stages/<n>_<name>/   stage.json {state: queued|running|done|failed|refused|lost|cancelled, argv, pid, started, ended, wall_s, exit_code,
                       knobs, inputs: [{path, sha256}], outputs: [{path, md5}], script_md5, log: "log.txt"}; the outputs themselves
  plans/               the plans used, byte copies of a frozen plan or new files with a provenance block
                       {"provenance": {"author": "owner"|"session"|"generator", "from": <path>, "from_sha256": …, "at": …,
                        "changes": [{"path": "/timeline/1/n", "old": 12, "new": 14}], "words": "<his words, verbatim>"}}
  annotations.json     [{id, still: "k0"|"A"|"B", kind: box|point|polygon|window, pts: [[x, y]…] (image px), image: {path, sha256, w, h},
                        role: lock|matte|local|glow|object|exclude|anchor, label, author, ts, note}]  (stale when the image sha256 differs)
  renders/<rid>/       loop.mp4 or transition.mp4, the gif, sheet.jpg, frames/ (JPEGs for the scrubber), frames.json, measure.json or
                       info.json, render.json {plan_path, plan_sha256, knobs, draft, script_md5, md5_mp4, md5_gif, wall_s}
  probes/<pair>/       the method strips and their numbers
  requests/            plan_<stamp>.json, skeptic_<stamp>.json, reading_<stamp>.json, director_<stamp>.json, keyframe_<stamp>.json
  answers/             what the session wrote back (a plan, skeptic_<n>.md + crops, readings.json, a director plan, helpers/)
  candidates/          <name> → a render id, the verdict (from the latest skeptic answer), judged_md5 (the md5 the verdict was given on)
  picks/               the grade files, each with the page md5 it was given on
  index.html, page.md5  the review page at the job root: loop_seq.py page reads <root>/in/<seq>/ and <root>/<seq>/<variant>/ and writes
                       keys.jpg into the prep folder, so a loop job keeps that layout (in/<seq>/ the prep, <seq>/<variant>/ the renders) and
                       stages/ holds the records pointing at those outputs: one home per output (lens 1 item 3)
  rounds/<n>/          a round = one page over one or more jobs (the Halloween page carried 10 sequences and 24 candidates): the
                       candidates listed, the page, its md5, his grade text verbatim, the parked candidates of earlier rounds (lens 2)
  owner_frames/        frames he marked
  remote_queue.json    the box's items {kind, prompt, seed, state, expected_s, started, ended, outputs}
```

Rules the data model enforces: a loop render passes `--set in=<job>/in/<seq>` so the plan's `in` is the job's prep, never the session's (lens 1 item 3); `prep` reads `.jpg` and `.jpeg` only, so an import of HEIC or PNG stills converts them into the job's `in/` first (the originals untouched); a run folder made before the studio existed (the angel, the seasons round 4) is adopted as a job by pointing at it, so his first screen can grade the two ungraded pages (lens 2); a render whose `judged_md5` differs from its current md5 shows "unjudged after a fix" (Track B C3), and the page endpoint refuses to build a page that holds an unjudged or NOT READY candidate unless the request says `override: true`, which the page then prints (C3, C7); a rerun whose outputs' md5s equal the previous run's says "unchanged" on its tile (C4); a rulings table in the registry (a knob, the value ruled out, the DECISIONS date) warns before a run that sets one (C8: `hole_reveal=true`); a new round's first render refuses until the previous round's grade text is saved, or the round is marked "ungraded by his word" (R3); a draft is a draft in its folder name and its chip (C6); a stage's outputs are written to a temporary name and renamed on success, so "exists" means "finished" (Track C §8); on start and on every read, a `running` stage whose pid is gone is reconciled from its outputs (done) or marked lost (the Fable review's fix, T60); the originals are symlinked and hashed, never copied (`AGENTS.md`); every plan file carries its provenance block and the frozen plans are never written (Track B R2, `ui_design.md §5`).

## 5. The backend contract

Every heavy call is a subprocess of `.venv/bin/python` with its stdout and stderr in the stage's `log.txt`; the server process imports neither tool nor any research script and uses numpy and cv2 only to turn arrays and mp4s into PNGs and JPEGs (the workbench's rule, `ui_design.md §4`, kept). The runner wraps every subprocess: it records the process group, the start time and the exit code in `stage.json`, writes the outputs into a `.part` folder and renames it on exit 0 (an output that exists is one that finished), writes a `done` marker, and cancels by `killpg` (lens 1 items 3 and 9). The registry is hand-written in `studio.py`: a knob's range is null unless the script's `--help` or `SCHEMA.md` states one (`transitions.py` states ranges for `--zoom`, `--color` and `--warp`; no loop key has a stated range), and a null-range knob is a number field with its default and sentence (lens 1 item 1). A "suggest" step reads the relation (Reveal's verdict, the transitions class, the number of stills, the motion's size) and proposes the flow and the first plan from the playbook's recipe table (`loops.md §4` as data), so a refused mismatch pair routes to the pair flow and a sequence to the loop flow (his "flexibly adapt"; UI3's suggestions panel, kept). ## 6. The visual design

`DESIGN.md` is normative (the tokens in its frontmatter; the light table; one sans family and a monospace for data; amber for the action, the selection and the running state; the meaning colours; flat regions with 1 px lines; motion 150–200 ms as state only). Three components carry the design: the **stage strip** (and the same component for a render's frames and a round's candidates), the **shared frame axis** (the scrubber and every per-frame plot on one x, a vertical line at the current frame, the skeptic's markers), and the **comparator** (Reveal's wipe re-hosted beside A/B, onion skin and difference, with 1:1 and 2× crops that do not blur). The incumbent pages are evidence of three unrelated styles (Track C §4: Reveal's warm brown with an italic serif wordmark; the workbench's 13 px Menlo on white; the review pages' light grey), replaced by one world. The build runs impeccable's detector on the served URL at a 2560×1440 viewport, then `audit`, `harden` (300 frames, a 40-key plan, the box offline), `clarify` (every refusal and error names the problem and the fix) and `polish` last or not at all (Track C §4; his 2026-10-20 "bare in terms of polishing"); the engine's first run downloads a binary (a dev-tool action his standing order "fetch anything you need" covers). Every text the studio writes for him (a page note, a chip's word, a refusal) and every document of the build session passes the no-slop pass (`.claude/rules/prose.md`).
