# Loop transitions A → B → A on the owner's two photos (2026-10-20; an Opus 5.5 subagent's track, its return message kept here by the main thread because the subagent's Write was refused)

Two loops are on `benchmarks/runs/2026-10-20/loop/index.html` (each an autoplaying mp4, a 540 px GIF and a contact sheet of every 3rd frame). Each loop is 59 frames at 30 fps on a 1588×1920 canvas; the last frame is A again. Inputs `in/A.jpg` (eyes forward) and `in/B.jpg` (eyes aside), copies of `~/Downloads/ihorr/1.jpg` and `2.jpg`.

| Loop | Leg 1 (A → B) | Leg 2 (B → A) | Max step | Mean step | Seam step | Face jerk max | Render s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| glide (ranked first by the numbers) | morph with seven hand anchors, falloff 0.25 | the same morph with `--warp 0.85 --color 0.5` (a little more linear, less colour pull) | 4.22 | 3.02 | 0.79 | 1.0 px | 11.0 + 8.5 |
| breathe | the same leg 1 plus a 5 % push-in centred on the duck (a post-process on the frames, endpoints untouched) | morph plus a 3 % push-in centred on the forehead (the return drifts upward, not a mirror) | 5.25 | 3.63 | 0.81 | 1.24 px | 11.1 + 8.7 |

Facts the track measured:
- The plain morph preset fails on this pair: SIFT finds 12 inliers (13 for B → A), under the 30 the class A path needs, so the tool falls to class B `saliency-panzoom` (median displacement 106 px), a cross-dissolve with two ducks and two pairs of eyes mid-clip. `--class A` forced gives `homography+dis` at mean certainty 0.041: the background tears at frames 6–21, max step 12.5–14.3.
- Seven hand anchors (canvas px, read by eye from gridded crops; the eye sockets, not the irises; the duck's head and base; the top of the hair; the rail's end cap and bracket) give the method `mls-anchors+falloff`: A → B `700,1285,520,1290;1150,1285,1035,1285;980,1265,800,1265;975,1372,788,1375;786,539,768,437;267,383,442,225;358,542,550,367`; B → A swaps each pair. Falloff 0.45 showed the rail twice mid-clip; 0.25 does not. Three more anchors (temples, headband) bent the rail and the lid edge and were dropped. YuNet returned only low-score boxes on the half-hidden face.
- Both legs use `--canvas common` (the default `finish` gives each leg a different canvas). `--camera` and `--zoom` do nothing once anchors are given. At crf 18 the seam step was 1.06 (above its neighbours 0.46 and 0.81); at crf 12 it is 0.79. A `flow-dissolve` leg 2 arrives at A still moving (last step 1.40 against 0.28) and was rejected.
- Leg-1 command: `.venv/bin/python transitions.py pair in/A.jpg in/B.jpg --out c4_rail_f0.25/leg1 --preset morph --seconds 1 --fps 30 --max-long 1920 --canvas common --anchors "<A→B>" --anchor-falloff 0.25`; the loops are joined by `work/build_loop.py` (imports `transitions.prepare` and `iter_frames`, leg 1 + leg 2 minus its first frame, raw endpoints asserted byte-equal, libx264 crf 12); measures by `work/measure.py` on the decoded mp4s.

Defects by the track's eye (UNVERIFIED by the owner): the headphone band on the left shows two arcs at frames 9–18; the gaze change is a cross-dissolve inside the eye sockets (milky irises at frames 12–15 and 43–46; no tool option moves an iris). The GIFs play about 10 % fast (33 ms stored as 30 ms). Nothing downloaded; `transitions.py` unchanged. Rejected variants under `c1..c5`, `leg2_*`, `work/` (55 MB, gitignored).
