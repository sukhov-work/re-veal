# Retrospective, 2026-10-20: why 18 graded pages produced one acceptance, and what holds for any pair

Verdict: the one accepted clip (mismatch_3 round 11, `benchmarks/runs/2026-10-17/director_r11/director_picks_11.json`) is the only clip on record where the camera never stops, one beat spans the clip, no B pixel appears through a hole, and the one dissolve inside it was asked for by the owner. Every other clip fails on two complaint families that no round closed and no measure reads: B's pixels shown before their beat (five rounds, the owner's first caveat on 2026-10-20) and motion that pauses or runs linear per layer (eight rounds, his second). The renderer has no rule for either; its timing is a smootherstep per layer with zero velocity at both window ends (`scripts/research/director_render.py:42-53`), and B's pixels enter the composite at eight places, each with its own default or knob (§3c below). The pair-specific primitives (the sun's recede, the wire into the streak, the radial sky) closed pair-specific faults and never a family. The record is 18 graded pages from 2026-09-13 to 2026-10-20: 6 of the transitions engine (TR), 7 of the layered family, the nested probe, 4 of the director; about 340 clips by 2026-09-26 (`audits/retrospective-2026-09-26.md §2`) and 17 director clips since (`benchmarks/2026-10-17-director.md`).

## 3. Why progress has been slow: the hypotheses against the record

(a) Each round fixes the previous grade with a pair-specific primitive or knob. Supported. The renderer grew from the 298-line probe (2026-10-17, DECISIONS second line) to 1,749 lines on 2026-10-18 (`wc -l`), by 8 fixes in round 8, 5 in round 9, 18 patches in round 10 and 10 in round 11 (`§13.11`, `§13.14`, `§13.17`, `§13.19`). The schema gained 7 items in round 9, 7 items with about 15 parameters in round 10, 12 items in round 11 (`plans/SCHEMA.md`, the three "Additions" sections). Of the eight transform kinds, `recede`, `line_to` and the sky block ran on one pair only (mismatch_5) and carry constants read off that photo: `limb_flatten` 0.55, `blob_L` 110, `radial_end` 1.6, `radial_near_at` 0.25 (SCHEMA round 10 and 11; T46 names the photo's own halo as the cause). The operator set 16 knobs by hand on round 8's six clips, 16 on round 9's three, 7 on round 10's three and 12 on round 11's three (the sheet's knob columns); the planner's own knob lists hold 3 per plan (`plans/*_r11.json`). The owner warned against this five times: 2026-09-26 ("be carefull also not to generalize"), 2026-09-27 three times ("do not overfit!"), 2026-10-20 ("never overfit on specific pictures or flows"). The rounds ran on three pairs with no held-out pair; one was asked for on 2026-10-17 (`§13.6`, `§13.10` "No held-out pair was named") and none arrived.

(b) The timing model is per-layer windows with per-layer eases and no global continuity. Supported. `ease01` is a smootherstep on `[t0, t1]` (`director_render.py:42-49`), `layer_e` applies it per layer (51-53), the camera uses its own smootherstep over the whole clip (1561). A smootherstep has zero velocity at both ends, so every beat starts from rest and stops dead, and sequential windows (the café: the tree 0.05–0.3, the lead 0.1–0.24 of the chain, k1 at 0.42, k3 at 0.6, k2 at 0.85; the sheet, round 11) produce the pauses he names. Measured by the skeptic on the graded café clip: 18 frames (54–71) where only the face box changes (step 2.3–3.9) while the rest of the frame reads 0.14–0.27, then the shirt alone at 72–82, then 7 still frames (`research/2026-10-18/skeptic_r11b.md`); the lead's speed goes 1.0 → 13.6 px per frame between adjacent frames. The owner: "all happen with weird pauses and in very linear way". On mismatch_5 the three beats have their own windows and mix windows (`plans/mismatch_5_r11.json`: 0.15–0.9, 0.12–0.7, 0.2–0.85), which is his "bridge move a bit discretely". The accepted clip has one beat and a camera that moves from frame 0 to frame 89, so no pause is visible (§7).

(d) Matched things are aligned by one anchor pair, never by shape. Supported. ## 4. The universal rules

Result: eleven rules hold for any pair; four are the open families. Each carries the quotes it comes from, an observable on a decoded mp4 (or on the renderer's own alphas, which is cheaper and exact), and the mechanism that violates it today. A beat is a transform's window in the plan.

1. No pixel of B is visible before its beat, except inside a region the plan declares early (the container's surround under a nested camera; a front layer the plan names). Quotes: "should never clip to underlaying next/last frame in advance (unless it was specific director intention" (10-20); "two girls … visible through the cut" (10-18); "hard cut silhuette revealing B underneath" (10-17); "duplicate final picture that is already there" (09-28); "next frame square borders" (09-13). Observable: a per-frame B-visibility map (from the renderer: every alpha that samples `Bf`, `Ff`, `B_inA`, `hole`, `a_fin`, `a_star`; from an mp4: pixels nearer to B-through-the-camera than to A-through-the-camera by more than 12 levels, outside the declared early region); its first non-zero frame for each B element must be at or after that element's beat start, and its share before the first beat must be 0. Violated by the eight entry points of §3c; on the café by `hole_reveal` and `end_window=[0.05,0.45]`; on the window by the front layer's fade-in from frame 0.

2. Motion is continuous: while any beat is open, no element and no camera is at rest, and no element's speed jumps. Quotes: "weird pauses … very linear way, looks too artificial and clunky" (10-20); "elements ( especially bridge move a bit discretely" (10-20); "too discreet and dirty" (09-28); "move in very linear manner" (09-26); "too linear and naive" (09-27); "too direct and rushed" (09-26). Observable: track each moving element (the renderer's own maps give the exact per-element displacement; on an mp4, DIS flow between adjacent frames inside the element's matte); the per-frame median speed per element must have no plateau below 0.5 px per frame longer than 3 frames inside [first beat start, last beat end] unless the camera moves there, and no frame where speed changes by more than 30 % of the clip's peak speed; over the whole frame, no run of more than 3 frames with mean step below 0.3 levels while a beat is open (skeptic_r11b measured 18 such frames and 7 still frames at the end). Violated by `ease01`/`layer_e` (zero velocity at every window end), by sequential windows, by the chain's matte-confined dissolves, and by `container.kind: none` with `parallax off` (no global motion to carry the pauses).

3. A moving subject deforms; it is never carried as a rigid cut-out. Quotes: "rough cutout of man from A is just moved" (10-17); "really ugly linear 2d move of cut man" (10-18); "like you cut out yellow man , move him onto man B" (10-20); "yellow man to rotate and gradually transform" (10-17); "cheap 2d cutout" (09-27). Observable: the element's appearance matched against its own frame-0 template (after removing the similarity): the match must fall below 0.95 by one third of its move (skeptic_r11b: 0.98–1.00 throughout the glide); its silhouette IoU with the start silhouette, similarity removed, below 0.9 by mid-move. Violated by the chain's lead (a similarity, 1132-1159), `exit` (a translation), `move_to`, `recede` (a scale).

4. Matched things align by shape and landmarks at the end of their beat, not by one centre. Quotes: "do not align (perfectly ) neither by the centers nor by shapes" (10-20); "a bit off center" (10-17); "image shifts first, then morphs" (09-13); "weird oval shape before settle" (10-17); "poorly aligned" (10-17). Observable: at the beat's end, the contour of A's element under the map against B's counterpart's contour: symmetric Chamfer distance ≤ 8 px RMS at 1920 px (the frontier report's criterion for the bridge, `research/2026-10-17/frontier_report.md` item 2); for a luminary, the radial luminance profile of the moved disc within 10 levels of B's at every radius. Violated by centre-and-radius anchors and the rotation-free lead (T34).

5. No edge exists in a frame that exists in neither photo. Quotes: "hard edges" (eleven pages, TR3 to D9); "borders and edges visible in intermediate frames" (09-28); "concentric bands" (10-18); "blurred square shape" (10-18); "sun limb still is not fully uniform" (10-20); "solid internal borders" (09-27). Observable: Canny edges of the frame not within 3 px of an edge of A or B brought through the frame's maps; their share ≤ 0.2 % of pixels; no circle or straight line among them longer than 40 px (Hough on the residual edges). Violated by the recede's rim ramp and plate circle, the radial sky window, the chain's matte (the hard shoulder edge at frames 76–78, skeptic_r11), the front's hard lower edge, and any hard-mask fallback (`info.json` `matte_fallbacks`).

6. When a subject changes, the whole subject changes, and the rest of the frame is not frozen. Quotes: "very jittery morphs only part of face … and nothing else" (10-18); "transform shape, transform face and finalize rest of image , all happen with weird pauses" (10-20). Observable: during a subject beat, the region with per-frame step above 1 level must cover the subject's whole matte (≥ 80 % of it), and the frame outside the subject must not read a step below 0.3 for longer than 3 frames (rule 2). Violated by the chain's union matte with a face-box guide and halved flow.

7. A dissolve is a beat only where the plan declares it, and then it takes its whole window; elsewhere no pixel shows A and B at once.
